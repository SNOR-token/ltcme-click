# Privacy Policy for LTCme.click

**Last Updated: September 2025**

## Introduction

LTCme.click ("we", "our", "the App") is a self-custody Litecoin wallet application. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you understand how your information is handled.

## Information We Collect

### No Personal Data Collection
- **We do NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information** such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.
- **We do NOT collect your seed phrases, private keys, or wallet addresses.**

### Self-Custody Model
- All wallet data, including your Litecoin addresses, transaction history, and balances, **remains solely on your device**.
- We have **zero access** to your funds or wallet information.
- Your seed phrase is generated and stored locally on your device only.

### Technical Data
The app may collect anonymous, non-personal technical data for functionality purposes:
- **Network connectivity status** (to check if you're online)
- **App usage analytics** (anonymous, aggregated data about feature usage)
- **Device information** (OS version, app version - used for compatibility)

## How We Use Information

- To provide wallet functionality (balances, transactions, address generation)
- To improve app performance and user experience
- To diagnose technical issues

## Data Storage

All your wallet data is stored **locally on your device**. We do not:
- Upload your data to any servers
- Share your data with third parties
- Store your data in the cloud (unless you explicitly enable optional cloud backup features)

## Third-Party Services

### Cloudflare (Hosting)
- Our web app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages
- Cloudflare may collect standard web server logs (IP addresses, user agents, referrers)
- These logs are **not linked to your wallet or identity**

### Stripe (Pro Subscriptions)
- If you purchase a Pro subscription, payment processing is handled by Stripe
- Stripe collects payment information directly from you
- We do not store or have access to your payment card details
- See [Stripe's Privacy Policy](https://stripe.com/privacy) for details

### Supabase (Optional Cloud Sync)
- If you enable cloud wallet sync, encrypted wallet data is stored in Supabase
- Data is encrypted end-to-end before leaving your device
- We cannot access your encrypted wallet data

## Security

- All communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption
- Sensitive data is encrypted locally on your device
- We follow industry best practices for cryptocurrency wallet security

## Children's Privacy

Our app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
