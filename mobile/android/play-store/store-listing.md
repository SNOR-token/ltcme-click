## Full Description

LTCme.click is the ultimate self-custody Litecoin wallet with an always-on AI companion. Manage your LTC with confidence.

### Key Features:

✅ **True Self-Custody** - You control your seed phrase, you control your funds. No one else can access your wallet.

✅ **Live Mainnet Balances** - Real-time balance updates directly from the Litecoin blockchain.

✅ **AI-Powered Companion** - Get intelligent insights about your wallet, transactions, and the Litecoin network.

✅ **Buy & Sell LTC** - Easy on-ramp and off-ramp directly in the app.

✅ **Multiple Wallets** - Create and manage multiple Litecoin wallets in one place.

✅ **Transaction History** - View all your incoming and outgoing transactions.

✅ **Address Book** - Save frequently used addresses for easy sending.

✅ **Pro Features** - Unlock advanced features with a Pro subscription:
   - Transaction risk analysis
   - Enhanced AI capabilities
   - Priority support
   - More free AI messages

✅ **Open Source** - Transparent code, community audited.

✅ **No Tracking** - We don't collect your personal data or wallet information.

### Security First

- Your seed phrase never leaves your device
- All sensitive data is encrypted locally
- No server can access your wallet
- Industry-standard security practices

### Supported Networks

- Litecoin Mainnet (LTC)

### What's New
